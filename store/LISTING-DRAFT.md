A full shell for your CardputerZero: terminal, browser, music, camera and more.

**Description:**
Optic Suite turns the CardputerZero's small screen into a complete, keyboard-driven
workspace. Everything is one app, drawn for 320x170 and usable without a mouse.

- Terminal: a real Linux shell with a font sized for the screen.
- Browser: a fast text browser with search; pages are reflowed to fit.
- Music: play your own files with cover art, search and play YouTube, and save
  songs to ~/Music. The volume keys (fn+S, fn+D) work while music plays.
- Camera: a viewfinder that opens in under a second, and a gallery for your shots.
- Sensors and Network: what the device sees, and Wi-Fi at a glance.
- HDMI: with the optional Optic DE package, start and stop a desktop on an HDMI
  screen from the app and see its mode and refresh rate.
- System: brightness, volume and the settings your device can actually change.

Optional tools are fetched or used when present: yt-dlp is downloaded the first
time you search for music; ffmpeg, curl and NetworkManager are used if installed.
